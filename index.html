Draft Night Special

ASFFL Successfully Conducts Draft Despite Lack of Stickers, Electricity and Meaningful Oversight

Ten managers, hundreds of Post-it notes and one pizza oven produce five suspiciously close Week 1 matchups.

Filed by The Shore Report Investigative Desk

AMHERST SHORE — Against overwhelming odds, the Amherst Shore Fantasy Football League completed its annual draft beside the ocean without official player stickers, professional supervision or any indication that Giants loyalty would be kept separate from roster strategy.

The league’s technical department—Nick, Lakota and Carlie—prepared hundreds of handwritten, color-coded Post-it notes and arranged them by average draft position. Managers then conducted detailed research by staring at the board and selecting whichever name seemed familiar.

After beginning indoors, league officials moved the operation to the bonfire, where Assistant General Manager Haleigh supervised the Sleezy Sandbars while Oliver operated a pizza oven. This made Haleigh the first assistant GM in league history and Oliver the first manager asked to oversee both Ja’Marr Chase and pizza dough at the same time.

Paul, the defending champion, did not attend. This did not prevent him from drafting a projected Week 1 favorite or from eventually offering everyone six trades involving the same three players.

A Brief and Selective History

The official champions are:

  • 2024: Grant, following an exhaustive audit.
  • 2025: Paul, following a successful hostile takeover of the league.
  • 2026: To be determined—although Ken probably already knows and simply claims he hasn’t looked into it.

Last year, Paul defeated Grant in the championship after Grant eliminated Kyle and Paul defeated Carlie in the semifinals. Kyle, Paul, Grant, Luc and Carlie all finished 10–4, proving that regular-season success is widely available and ultimately meaningless.

Oliver finished 2–12. Sarah finished 4–10 but retained full control of the family, making her the league’s most powerful executive regardless of record.

The last-place team has since left the league, creating the disturbing impression that relegation may already exist.

Draft Night Investigations

Ken Denies Conducting Research

Big Apple manager Ken once again insists that he completed no preparation and merely selected players according to the board. Investigators remain skeptical.

Ken somehow left with Christian McCaffrey, Derrick Henry, Lamar Jackson, Tony Pollard and Brian Thomas Jr. League officials describe the odds of accidentally assembling that roster while reading every Giants article published this offseason as “suspiciously Ken-like.”

This may be an inherited family trait. Ken and Kyle’s mother was famous for announcing that she had never played a particular game before, immediately understanding all the rules and then somehow winning.

No formal charges have been filed.

Lakota Is No Longer a Beginner

One year after entering the league without much fantasy experience, Lakota arrived wearing a custom Giant Expectations shirt, carrying evidence of actual preparation and possibly planning several moves in advance.

She drafted James Cook, CeeDee Lamb, Kyren Williams, Justin Herbert, Sam LaPorta and other people good enough to make everyone uncomfortable.

Nick helped Lakota prepare through numerous mock drafts. He may now discover why defense contractors generally avoid training the opposition.

Grant Completes Annual Roster Audit

Grant quietly drafted Jonathan Taylor, Omarion Hampton, George Pickens, Ladd McConkey, D’Andre Swift, Mark Andrews and enough depth to survive a moderate regional emergency.

The future CPA has reportedly classified every player as either an asset, a liability or “pending further review.”

As usual, Grant appears to have one of the league’s strongest teams on paper. As usual, the rest of the league will wait for injuries to perform the work we cannot.

Carlie Denies Receiving Inside Information

Carlie followed last year’s semifinal appearance by drafting Bijan Robinson, Chase Brown, Nico Collins, Zay Flowers, Drake Maye, Rome Odunze and Marvin Harrison Jr. This appears to be another strong team.

Questions have naturally arisen about how much advice Carlie received from Grant, her longtime partner, housemate and one of the league’s most experienced managers.

Carlie maintains that she listens to Grant’s recommendations before making her own decisions. Grant has reportedly established a strict information barrier between the two franchises, reviewed the barrier himself and determined that it complies with all applicable accounting standards.

The couple’s two dogs declined to comment.

Luc’s Draft Remains Under Review

Luc selected Amon-Ra St. Brown, De’Von Achane, Jordan Love, DeVonta Smith and Travis Etienne, leading experts to conclude that he knew exactly what he was doing.

Other selections led those same experts to conclude that he may have briefly wandered away from the bonfire.

The investigation is ongoing, although Luc’s status as the league’s only Cowboys fan already constitutes substantial evidence against him.

Sarah Establishes the ASFFL’s Giants Employment Program

Sarah drafted Saquon Barkley and A.J. Brown before securing Giants rookies Cam Skattebo and Jaxson Dart, followed by the Giants defense.

League analysts remain divided over whether this was a fantasy-football strategy or simply an effort to ensure that Sunday’s lineup contains enough familiar faces.

Either way, Dartastic will be prepared each week—and no Giant will ever lack unconditional support.

Week 1: Five Games, No Safe Predictions

The league website projects every opening matchup to be close. All five are within six points, and one is separated by less than a single point.

This either promises an extraordinary opening weekend or proves the computer has no idea what it is doing.

Quack Quack vs. Giant Expectations

Kyle enters Week 1 with Jayden Daniels, Jahmyr Gibbs, Brock Bowers, Drake London and several years more fantasy experience than his opponent.

Lakota enters with CeeDee Lamb, James Cook, Kyren Williams and the ability to learn alarmingly fast.

The computer gives Kyle a slight advantage. This is exactly the sort of matchup Kyle must win because losing would invalidate several decades of unsolicited fantasy advice.

What’s at stake: Experience, family bragging rights and Kyle’s remaining credibility.

Drake & Bake vs. Dartastic

Carlie assembled a serious roster led by Bijan Robinson, Nico Collins, Drake Maye and Zay Flowers.

Sarah counters with Saquon Barkley, A.J. Brown, Mike Evans and a collection of Giants who will receive her full support whether the projections agree or not.

On paper, Carlie has the advantage. Sarah, however, has the benefit of being the family matriarch and therefore remains in charge regardless of the final score.

What’s at stake: Careful roster construction versus unwavering faith in the New York Giants.

Bam Bam Cam vs. Smack Dak ASB

Nick drafted Puka Nacua, Justin Jefferson, Josh Allen, RJ Harvey, DK Metcalf—and, for reasons known only to Nick, Patrick Mahomes as a backup quarterback.

Luc counters with Amon-Ra St. Brown, Achane, Jordan Love, DeVonta Smith and Dak Prescott.

This matchup features an experienced sports fan with two superstar quarterbacks against a Cowboys fan whose team name contains both Dak and ASB. Nobody is entirely sure which description belongs to the favorite.

What’s at stake: Nick’s mock-draft preparation against Luc’s bonfire instincts.

Sleezy Sandbars vs. Big Apple

Week 1 delivers the league’s first father–son showdown as Oliver’s Sleezy Sandbars face Ken’s Big Apple.

Oliver’s roster features Ja’Marr Chase, Ashton Jeanty, Malik Nabers, Josh Jacobs, Davante Adams, Joe Burrow and George Kittle.

Ken’s roster features Christian McCaffrey, Derrick Henry, Lamar Jackson and the insistence that all of it happened randomly.

The projection favors Oliver by less than one point—the closest matchup of the week. Assistant GM Haleigh is expected to monitor the situation while Oliver focuses on team morale, social engagements and pizza.

Ken, meanwhile, will attempt to defeat his oldest son while maintaining that he has no idea how any of this works.

What’s at stake: Father–son bragging rights, household hierarchy and whether Oliver can equal half of last year’s win total by Sunday night.

Boot Tuten Boogie vs. Paul’s Team Name Available for Trade

The defending finalists meet immediately in Week 1.

Grant brings the league’s most meticulously balanced roster. Paul brings Jalen Hurts, Jonathan Taylor—wait, Grant has Jonathan Taylor—Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Kenneth Walker, Trey McBride, Chris Olave and an established willingness to trade anything that is not physically attached to the house.

Paul is currently projected to win by fewer than three points.

What’s at stake: A championship rematch and approximately 14 trade offers by Tuesday morning.
