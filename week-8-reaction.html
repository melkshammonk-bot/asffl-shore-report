The Shore Report: Week 8 — MAKE FANTASY FOOTBALL GREAT AGAIN!

An Election Day Special — Tremendous Analysis. Unbelievable Scores. Very Questionable Management.

Tuesday Reaction · November 3, 2026
Guest Columnist: Donald Trump (Fictional Parody)

Editor's note: This is fictional satire. Donald Trump did not write or endorse this article.

Nobody knows fantasy football better than me. Nobody. I've been told this by some very smart people. Tremendous people. They say, "Sir, how do you understand fantasy football so well?" And I tell them, it's a gift. A tremendous gift.

I've looked at this league. Mostly Canadians. Wonderful people, the Canadians. Very polite. Almost too polite. They apologize when they beat you. I've never apologized for winning. Not once.

They tell me these people can't even vote in our election. Can you believe it? I'm doing this tremendous column, maybe the greatest sports column ever written, and they can't vote. Very strange arrangement. But they can vote on trades. Terrible trades, by the way. I've seen better deals negotiated by a vending machine.

But enough about Canada. We're here to talk football. American football. The greatest football. And I've got a plan. A tremendous plan.

WE'RE GOING TO MAKE FANTASY FOOTBALL GREAT AGAIN!

And after looking at these results, I can tell you some of these managers are going to need a lot of help.

SARAH WINS! WE DID IT! I DID IT!

Folks, this is historic. Nobody thought it could happen. Seven weeks. Seven losses. People were saying, "Sir, Sarah may never win a fantasy football game." Very sad people. Very negative.

And then I arrive.

Dartastic 133.40 — Big Apple 128.30.

Coincidence? I don't believe in coincidences. I believe in results.

Sarah got 20.90 from Hubbard, 16.30 from Rice, and 18 beautiful points from the Washington defense. She actually dropped the Giants defense to pick up Washington. A Giants fan! That's like selling your own family to buy a better family. Very tough decision. Tremendous courage.

Meanwhile, Ken had Pollard scoring 27.70, Michael Wilson with 25.80, and Harold Fannin with 21.40. Great numbers. Beautiful numbers.

And he lost.

Why? Lamar Jackson. Zero. Chris Godwin. Zero. Two players. Zero points. I've seen better production from an unplugged toaster.

Ken, I know you're a businessman. You understand value. But when you pay for two players and receive absolutely nothing, that's not a bargain. That's a timeshare.

Sarah wins by 5.10. I'd normally demand a recount on a margin that small. But not today. Today we celebrate.

Sarah, you're welcome.

LAKOTA IS RUNNING THIS LEAGUE. NICK SHOULD BE INVESTIGATED.

Giant Expectations 144.85 — Drake & Bake 126.15.

Seven wins. One loss. Six consecutive victories.

Lakota is winning so much that people are getting tired of winning. I hear it all the time. "Sir, please, can somebody else win?" And I say no. Not until we've finished winning.

Tee Higgins scored 37.50. Bucky Irving added 20. Tremendous production.

But here's what concerns me.

Lakota started Matthew Stafford. Nine-point-eight-five. On her bench? Kirk Cousins. THIRTY-POINT-SIX-FIVE.

She left almost 21 additional points sitting there doing absolutely nothing. And still won by the largest margin of the week.

That's not fantasy football. That's showing off.

And I'm told Nick helped Lakota prepare for this league through mock drafts. Nick! You trained her! You gave her the tools! Now she's sitting at 7–1, looking down at everybody.

This is why I don't train my competitors. I train winners. Specifically, me.

Carlie, meanwhile, got 23.45 from Drake Maye and 19.90 from Matthew Golden. Zay Flowers contributed absolutely nothing.

Zero. A terrible number. Nobody likes zero. Except Ken, apparently. He's collecting them.

NICK WINS. GRANT'S BENCH DEMANDS A TRADE.

Bam Bam Cam 155.25 — Boot Tuten Boogie 138.95.

Nick scored 155.25. Highest score in the league. Josh Allen gave him 29.45, Puka Nacua 22, and Jacksonville's defense scored 24.

Twenty-four points from a defense! I've seen entire offenses that couldn't do that. Some of them play for the New York Giants.

But Grant? Grant has a problem.

D'Andre Swift scored 26.90. Very good. And Jonathan Taylor, a big name, a tremendous name, gave him 3.70.

Three-point-seven.

Meanwhile, Emmett Johnson was sitting on Grant's bench with 24.30.

I looked at those numbers and said, "Who is managing this team? Is there an adult in the room?"

Grant, you don't put your best people on the bench. You put them in the game. It's a very simple concept. I've been explaining it for years.

Nick improves to 5–3. Grant falls to 4–4.

Grant's bench has requested a meeting with management. Management has declined.

KYLE WINS DESPITE KYLE. VERY IMPRESSIVE.

Quack Quack 147.10 — I Love Pitts 134.35.

Kyle won. Tremendous. But I've reviewed his lineup, and frankly, I'm not convinced Kyle had anything to do with it.

Bowers scored 27.20. Gibbs scored 23.80. Braelon Allen added 22.30.

Strong players. Beautiful players. Winners.

And then there's Drake London. One-point-eight. Parker Washington. Three-point-six.

I've had golf scores higher than that. On individual holes. Very difficult holes, by the way.

And Adonai Mitchell? Twenty-seven-point-two points.

On the bench.

Kyle, what are we doing here?

You have a player scoring 27 points and you're keeping him on the sidelines like he's waiting for a bus. Incredible.

Fortunately for Kyle, Luc had a few problems of his own. Bhayshul Tuten scored 29.90. Pittsburgh's defense added 27. Excellent.

And Kyle Pitts, the man who supplies half of Luc's franchise name, scored 1.60.

One-point-six!

Now I'm told the team is called I Love Pitts because he has a running back named Love and a tight end named Pitts. Very clever. Two names. One franchise. Tremendous branding.

But here's the problem. Pitts isn't producing. And Love isn't saving him.

I've seen businesses built around two people before. When one stops performing, you have to make changes. Sometimes you fire somebody. Sometimes you change the sign.

Maybe next year it's I Tolerate Pitts. Maybe Love Is Complicated. Maybe We're Seeing Other Tight Ends.

I'm just trying to help.

Kyle reaches 4–4. Luc drops to 2–6. Both managers have been offered counseling. Very expensive counseling. The best.

OLIVER DEFEATS PAUL. THE EXPERTS ARE STUNNED. ESPECIALLY PAUL.

Sleezy Sandbars 142.90 — The Trading Block 128.55.

This was a tremendous upset. A historic upset. People are calling it the biggest thing to happen on Election Day. I haven't checked, but many people are saying it.

Oliver had Joe Burrow. Forty-four-point-one points.

Forty-four!

Ja'Marr Chase added 29.10. That's 73.20 points from two players. I did the math myself. Very quickly. People were amazed.

Paul had Smith-Njigba with 23.80 and McBride with 20.20. Respectable. But not enough.

And I want to discuss Paul's bench.

Minus 1.65 points.

Negative points! I've been around sports a long time. I've seen bad benches. I've seen terrible benches. But a bench that actually owes you points? That's something special.

Paul is probably already negotiating a trade with somebody. That's what he does. He sees a problem, he makes a deal. Tremendous activity. Whether the deals are any good, that's a different question.

Oliver and Paul are both 6–2 now. Paul stays second on total points. Oliver sits third.

And Haleigh, I understand you have some influence over this operation. Very smart. Very capable. I would strongly recommend keeping Burrow and Chase happy. Whatever they're asking for, give it to them.

Except the television remote. Never surrender the remote. That's how they get you.

THE STANDINGS. I'VE REVIEWED THEM PERSONALLY.

Lakota is 7–1. Tremendous. Paul and Oliver are 6–2. Nick is 5–3. Kyle and Grant are 4–4.

Everybody else has some work to do.

Some teams scored a lot of points this week. Others scored very few. Frankly, embarrassing numbers. Numbers so low I wouldn't even put them on a scoreboard. And believe me, I know scoreboards. The best scoreboards.

And the injuries? A disaster. We need healthy players. Strong players. Players who don't leave the game after three snaps. We're going to have the healthiest fantasy league anyone has ever seen. People said it couldn't be done.

That's the first part of my plan to Make Fantasy Football Great Again. Healthy players. Big scores. No more zeroes. Especially for Ken. We're going to get him some points. Tremendous points.

I've also been informed that this league has commissioners. Three of them. Kyle, Nick, and Oliver.

Three commissioners for ten teams. That's a lot of government. A tremendous amount of government. I'm going to look into it. We're going to make this league so efficient you won't believe it.

And one last thing. I understand these Canadians are going to keep playing this game all winter. In Canada. Very cold. Tremendous snow. I've seen pictures. I don't know how they do it.

But I respect it.

Next week we're going to win. Everybody's going to win. Even the teams that lose. Especially the teams that lose. That's how good this league is going to be.

Thank you. God bless the ASFFL.

MAKE FANTASY FOOTBALL GREAT AGAIN!
