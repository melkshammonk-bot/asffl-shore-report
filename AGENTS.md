# Shore Report editorial continuity

The maintained editorial reference is
`../ontap-press/editorial/asffl/league-bible.md`, including its publication-backed
lore ledger. Read it and the companion editorial rules before drafting. Weekly
anecdotes and private edition snapshots live under `../ontap-press/.private/`.
Conversation history is not a substitute for those maintained sources.

During each authorized publication, compare the approved article with the lore
ledger. Preserve retained new recurring material with its character, premise,
first edition/link, status and continuity boundaries. Keep verified league events,
real personal facts, fictional traits and one-week storylines separate. Unused
brainstorming stays unused. Mark planned material published only after confirming
the live edition; record its commit/URL or a no-new-lore check in the private
publication record. Do not alter old hashed snapshots.

Keep publication copy free of private audits, draft labels and private links.
Drafting does not authorize publication, commits, pushes or messages. Follow the
user's explicit publication instructions and stage only intended files.
