THE PYLON’S LINES: WEEK 5

October 9, 2026 · Gordie “The Pylon” MacNeil
Senior Road Crew Stop/Slow Technician

Right. Bit late this week.

Fella relieving me said he just had to pick up a turkey for the long weekend. Apparently he was raising it.

Finally get relieved, go to cross the road to my truck, and there’s a whole line of cars sitting there. I’m waving them through and getting increasingly disappointed in the provincial driving standards. Then a woman rolls her window down and says, “You might want to turn your sign around, dear.”

Still had STOP facing them. Held myself at work another twenty minutes. Woman gave me a coffee. I thanked her and told her we were doing our best.

Anyway. Thursday’s football has happened. Those points are already in; these are my picks from where we stand Friday. I won’t be taking credit for identifying George Pickens after he’s finished.

The Pylon’s Matchup of the Week

Giant Expectations (Lakota, 3–1) vs. The Trading Block (Paul, 4–0)

My line: Giant Expectations -2½
My winner: Giant Expectations

First against second. Paul hasn’t lost, and Lakota has just put up the biggest score in the league. Giant Expectations, not Giant Exaggerations. There’s evidence now.

She has already had a considerable Thursday. Bucky Irving supplied 31½ points, which was helpful because CeeDee Lamb managed only 1.90 while dealing with a quad injury. That leaves her with 33.40 in the bank and quite a bit still to do.

Paul’s difficulty is that Carolina and Kansas City have taken the weekend off. Tetairoa McMillan and Kenneth Walker are unavailable on byes, along with Bryce Young. Paul has put Hurts back in, paid thirteen dollars for Romeo Doubs and put Doubs straight to work. Smith-Njigba and McBride remain quite capable of making this look like an unnecessary discussion.

Still, this is a useful week to meet the champion. Two of last week’s biggest contributors cannot help him, and Irving has already done more than his share for Lakota.

Neither side has a clean medical sheet. Higgins and Addison are questionable for Lakota, Moore still carries a question mark, and Paul is waiting on Kamara. I’m taking Lakota narrowly on the present selections, with those decisions still outstanding.

She also has Stafford, Cook, Kyren and Mevis waiting for Monday night, with Moore in that same game if available. Paul’s current lineup will have finished by then. Whatever lead he builds Sunday, he’ll have to sit through Thanksgiving while Lakota keeps finding another player.

I’d save some pie. Could be a long evening.

Sleezy Sandbars (Oliver, 3–1) vs. Boot Tuten Boogie (Grant, 1–3)

My line: Boot Tuten Boogie -17½
My winner: Boot Tuten Boogie

Pickens has supplied twenty-eight points before I’ve even got the column in. Good start for Grant. At least one of us was ready Thursday.

Oliver got 3.90 from Ferguson and five from Aubrey on Thursday. That’s two positions finished without much protection against what Grant has already banked.

The remaining problem is availability. Chase is still questionable after the concussion, Jeanty is questionable too, and Monangai is out. Oliver won through Chase’s injury last week; asking him to manage another weekend around it, with less help elsewhere, is a different job.

Burrow and Chase together can change this quickly if Chase plays. Nabers, Adams and Kittle give Oliver other routes back. But I prefer Grant’s Taylor and Swift this week, particularly with Monangai unavailable in Chicago.

Grant’s starting Taylor Swift at running back. After losing to Carlie by fifty, he’s hoping to Shake It Off. Under this newspaper’s ruling, he’ll have to Walk It Off. Twice a day. Both dogs.

Shough throwing to Juwan Johnson also gives Grant a way to build on Thursday’s start.

Oliver has already exceeded last season’s two wins. I’m expecting the celebration to pause at three while Grant gets back to something resembling normal business.

I Love Pitts (Luc, 1–3) vs. Big Apple (Ken, 2–2)

My line: Big Apple -8½
My winner: Big Apple

Ken shops the waiver wire like he shops Harrisons in Amherst. Goes in for a washer, comes out with a sump pump and a slot receiver. Says he saved forty dollars. Nobody’s established what he went in there to fix.

“At that price, you can’t leave it there.”

This week it was six dollars for Jakobi Meyers. Luc dropped him, Ken bought him, and now Meyers is starting against Luc. I can see how that would be difficult to walk past.

This particular purchase has an identifiable job, too. Ken dropped Michael Pittman, who is out, and has Meyers available for Sunday’s early game. He also added Malachi Fields as a free agent, dropping Brian Thomas Jr.

Thomas could have won Ken last week’s matchup from the bench. Ken has addressed this embarrassing oversight by removing the witness.

Raymond remains on the bench. The twenty-seven-dollar receipt from last week has not yet become a starting requirement.

Lamar is out, so Purdy takes over at quarterback. McCaffrey and Henry are still the main reason I like Ken; the bargain aisle is supplementary.

Luc has Dak’s 15.80 already recorded and has traded Garrett Wilson to Kyle for Tuten and Downs, both now starting. Amon-Ra and Jameson give him a substantial interest in Detroit’s afternoon. There’s enough there to make Ken work for it.

Meanwhile, I Love Pitts currently has Pitts on the bench and Hockenson starting. The affection remains. Selection is a separate matter.

Ken by eight and a half. I’m assessing the football team. I haven’t inspected the sump pump.

Quack Quack (Kyle, 2–2) vs. Dartastic (Sarah, 0–4)

My line: Quack Quack -18½
My winner: Quack Quack

Kyle won Best Manager and Below Expectation in the same week. Having established that he could arrange the disappointment almost perfectly, he has decided to change some of it.

Garrett Wilson is in, with Tuten and Downs sent to Luc. Kyle has also found a three-dollar Keaton Mitchell. Good to establish he knows where that end of the price list is.

The forty-two-dollar Braelon Allen remains in the lineup, and with Breece Hall out there is still a football reason for the purchase. Gibbs, Bowers, London and the new Wilson give Kyle enough strength that I’m prepared to trust him again. This concerns the team only. It is not permission to resume giving everybody advice.

Sarah is still waiting for her first win and has had very little assistance from the injury report. Barkley is out; Rice and Hubbard are on bye. Jones and Skattebo are starting at running back, Hunter Henry is in the flex, and Friday’s addition Malik Washington goes straight into the receivers.

Goff gives her a proper chance to answer Kyle’s Gibbs through the same Detroit offense. She’ll need that passing game, plus something substantial from Evans and her tight ends. Dowdle’s questionable status is another decision to watch.

I’m taking Kyle by eighteen and a half. Sarah beating the reigning Best Manager would be a perfectly acceptable use of my only wrong pick.

Drake & Bake (Carlie, 2–2) vs. Bam Bam Cam (Nick, 2–2)

My line: Drake & Bake -2½
My winner: Drake & Bake

Carlie beat Grant by fifty with Drake Maye on the bench. Maye is back in this week. Darnold has gone, and a dollar bought C.J. Stroud for the reserve job. Drake & Bake has restored the Drake without issuing an apology for how well the temporary arrangement worked.

Nick already has 27.40 from Javonte Williams and Egbuka. That’s useful, but Carlie still has her whole lineup to come, including Bijan, Chase Brown and Nico Collins. I like that group to keep her close enough for Maye to matter.

Flowers is questionable and will be without Lamar if he plays. Nick has Jefferson’s questionable ankle to monitor. Neither manager should mistake a name currently in the lineup for a completed availability check.

This could follow everyone into Monday night. With Kelce on bye, Nick has Kincaid starting alongside Allen and Puka in that last NFL game. Carlie’s current team finishes Sunday. She’ll want a lead big enough that every completed pass doesn’t interrupt Thanksgiving.

I’m giving her two and a half again. Fifty was very generous, but she needn’t do all my work for me.

That’s Lakota, Grant, Ken, Kyle and Carlie on the clipboard.

Enjoy the long weekend. If you see me beside the road waving you through, check the sign. Apparently that part is binding.
