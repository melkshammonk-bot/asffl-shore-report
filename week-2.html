The Pylon’s Lines: Week 2

September 17, 2026 · Gordie “The Pylon” MacNeil, Senior Road Crew Stop/Slow Technician

Right. Gordie here. The foreman’s gone somewhere with the truck, my relief hasn’t shown, and I’ve had time to look over your teams.

I’ve set the lines. Don’t ask me for a spreadsheet. I’ve got a clipboard, a pencil and a fairly good idea who’s going to make this difficult.

These are Shore Report predictions, not sportsbook odds or gambling advice. If you’re putting money on something I worked out beside a culvert, give your head a shake.

The Pylon’s Matchup of the Week

Bam Bam Cam vs. The Trading Block

My line: The Trading Block -2½
My winner: The Trading Block

Now here’s a proper matchup. Nick and Paul both won last week, and nobody who won scored more than these two. Two heavy loads approaching one lane. Somebody’s backing up, and I don’t expect either of them to volunteer.

Nick’s got Josh Allen. Paul’s got Kenneth Walker. Both did some kind of work last week. I’ve gone back and forth on this one enough that the sign’s getting warm.

Then I hear Nick slept through Monday Night Football. Lakota caught him. You start the league and then sleep through the scouting? That’s foreman behaviour, Nick. You’re not supposed to learn that until you’ve got the truck keys.

I’m taking Paul by a couple and a bit. The man spent last Sunday telling Grant his team was dead while it was busy beating him. I’ve worked with fellas like that. “She’ll never start,” they say, already halfway down the road.

Quack Quack vs. Big Apple

My line: Quack Quack -7½
My winner: Quack Quack

Ken says he doesn’t prepare. Fine. I don’t prepare either. I just happen to know where every pothole is and which fellow’s going to drive straight into it.

Kyle needs this one. Last week he waited all Monday night for Waddle to do something, and I believe a person deserves a little compensation for sitting through that.

Bowers has a questionable flag for Kyle; Brian Thomas has one for Ken. That means check on them, not start composing a eulogy.

I’ve got Kyle by a touchdown and a bit. He’s down in Sarasota, so he’ll tell you the conditions are excellent. Of course they are, Kyle. Some of us have to hold the sign with both hands.

Sleezy Sandbars vs. Drake & Bake

My line: Drake & Bake -10½
My winner: Drake & Bake

Oliver’s got three tight ends in there. Three. That’s a committee. Before anyone catches a pass they’ll need a meeting, somebody will want minutes, and the afternoon’s gone.

Carlie won last week. Oliver did not. I could dress that up, but we’re already behind schedule.

There are questionable flags on both sides—Burrow and Kittle for Oliver, Collins and Flowers for Carlie—so nobody should be getting comfortable yet.

Still, I’m laying ten and a half with Carlie. Oliver can appeal from the Centre of the Universe. Tell him to allow six to eight weeks for a reply. We’re waiting on a part.

Boot Tuten Boogie vs. Giant Expectations

My line: Giant Expectations -3½
My winner: Giant Expectations

Grant scored a pile last week and still lost. He’ll have the figures. I don’t need them. I know the expression on a man who’s done everything properly and is about to explain it to you.

Lakota won, and I like a person who arrives in a team shirt. Shows commitment. Half our crew can’t find the vest they were wearing at lunch.

McConkey and Swift are marked questionable for Grant. Could both be fine. Could mean another long afternoon looking at his paperwork. We’ll check before the NFL games start.

I’ve got Lakota by three and a half. I had it at three, but Grant strikes me as the sort who’d dispute a tie until we lost the light.

Smack Dak ASB vs. Dartastic

My line: Smack Dak ASB -12½
My winner: Smack Dak ASB

Sarah’s got an empty receiver spot in the lineup I was handed. Temporary, I expect. I’m allowing for Pierce coming off the bench when I set this line—not saying that’s what she’ll do. Sarah runs her own operation.

She’s a devoted Giants supporter who drafted Barkley and Brown. I’m told Brown being a Patriot now was part of the explanation. Fair enough. I’ve painted over an old company name on a truck myself. You can still see it when the sun hits sideways.

Luc’s the Cowboys man. Normally that would finish the discussion, but I’m required to let the occasional questionable vehicle through.

I’ve got Luc by twelve and a half, with Sarah’s receiver vacancy treated as filled. If she beats him, she won’t need a speech from me. He’ll have quite enough to listen to.

That’s the sheet. One of you will tell me I’m wrong before Thursday’s NFL game has even started. Get in line. The foreman was here first.
