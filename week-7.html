THE PYLON’S LINES: WEEK 7

October 24, 2026 · Gordie “The Pylon” MacNeil
Senior Road Crew Stop/Slow Technician

The column’s late because I was holding the STOP sign. Nobody told me to turn it around. Seventeen years without a workplace incident, and suddenly I’m the problem.

I’ve lived in the Maritimes all my life. If a man’s standing beside unfinished work, I generally assume he’s been told to.

Chicago beat New England Thursday. Those points have already happened. I’m picking from where we stand Saturday evening; I won’t be arriving after the paving truck and claiming I laid the asphalt.

Last week I had three winners and two explanations. Carlie was one of the explanations. I’ve given her another line because I believe in people, and because the pencil still works.
