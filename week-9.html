The Pylon's Lines — Week 9: Improvement Required

By Gordie "The Pylon" MacNeil · November 5, 2026

Went into the editor's office Monday about that job posting I found in last week's paper. My job, apparently. Same hours, same responsibilities. Only difference was the advertisement said "experience preferred."

I've been standing on the side of the road holding a Stop/Slow sign for 23 years. Not sure what additional experience they're looking for. Maybe someone who's been hit.

Editor told me not to worry. Said I wasn't being fired. Said I was being placed on a Performance Improvement Plan.

I asked if there was a raise involved.

There wasn't.

Apparently my predictions aren't accurate enough, my readers aren't engaged enough, and my attitude could use improvement.

Three objectives. All in writing. He even made me sign it.

I told him I went three for five last week. He said that wasn't good enough. I reminded him that three out of five is 60 percent. That's a passing grade in most schools around here.

He said this wasn't school.

I said that explained why nobody was learning anything.

He's given me until the end of November to show improvement. I asked what happens if I don't.

He said we'd discuss my future.

I'm 63 years old. I've been discussing my future since 1987. Hasn't improved much.

Anyway, five games this week. I intend to get every one of them right. The editor says I need a more positive attitude, so I'm positive about that.

Whether the teams cooperate is another matter.

Bam Bam Cam (5–3) vs. Dartastic (1–7)

Sarah finally won a football game last week. Seven straight losses, then a victory. Whole family must have been delighted.

This week she plays her son.

Nick has Josh Allen, Puka Nacua, Justin Jefferson, and enough spare players to operate a second franchise. He's also got two Pittsburgh players in his starting lineup despite Pittsburgh having the week off. Maybe he knows something the NFL doesn't.

Sarah's got a Pittsburgh player starting too. Apparently it's hereditary.

The editor says I need to engage with my readers, so here's a question: If your mother beats you at fantasy football, do you still have to go over for Sunday dinner?

The Pylon's Line: Nick by 12.

Hope Sarah wins. Would be nice for her. There. Positive attitude.

Drake & Bake (2–6) vs. Big Apple (3–5)

Ken lost last week with two players scoring zero. He's made some lineup changes, which seems a reasonable response to employing people who contribute less than the furniture.

Carlie's getting Nico Collins back in the lineup. Ken's getting McCaffrey and Purdy back from their bye. Both teams are looking healthier, though half Ken's roster is still listed as questionable.

There's a word for a roster full of questionable people. Around here we call it the road crew.

Carlie has been making her own decisions all season, despite considerable interest from the Grant household in how those decisions get made.

I respect that. My wife makes her own decisions too. Made one about me in 1989. Still living with it.

The Pylon's Line: Carlie by 6.

Boot Tuten Boogie (4–4) vs. I Love Pitts (2–6)

Grant left 24 points on his bench last week while Jonathan Taylor gave him less than four. He's made some changes, but I notice the paperwork appears to be moving faster than the results.

Luc has a team named after two players: Love and Pitts. Love's on injured reserve, and Pitts scored 1.60 last week.

That's not a football team. That's a country song.

Luc also has Pittsburgh's defense starting during Pittsburgh's bye. Three days into my improvement plan and I'm already concerned about other people's performance.

The editor says I'm supposed to find something positive to say about every matchup.

Luc has a very nice team name.

The Pylon's Line: Grant by 14.

Quack Quack (4–4) vs. The Trading Block (6–2)

Kyle scored 147 points last week and still managed to leave another 27 on his bench. He's apparently decided to keep doing that.

Paul lost to Oliver and has been asking people to explain their reasoning ever since.

"Based on what?" he says.

Fair question. I've been asking the editor the same thing about my performance review.

Kyle's got Gibbs, Bowers and enough talent to beat anybody. Paul's got a better record, stronger season numbers, and a trading habit that would make a used-car salesman uncomfortable.

Both teams have something to prove.

Paul wants to prove last week was a fluke. Kyle wants to prove he knows what he's doing.

One of those seems considerably more difficult.

The Pylon's Line: Paul by 7.

GAME OF THE WEEK

Sleezy Sandbars (6–2) vs. Giant Expectations (7–1)

First place against third. Thirteen wins between them. Lakota's won six straight, and Oliver just knocked off Paul, who was sitting at the top of the league before last week.

This is the kind of matchup that gets people talking. The editor says I need more reader engagement. Well, here you go. Something worth engaging with.

Oliver's got Burrow throwing to Chase. Lakota's got Higgins catching passes from the same quarterback. Every Cincinnati touchdown is going to be a family argument waiting to happen.

Haleigh might want to arrange separate seating before kickoff. She's already got enough experience managing difficult personalities.

Lakota left Kirk Cousins and his 30 points on the bench last week and won anyway. I can't decide whether that's confidence or carelessness. Either way, it's working.

I've studied this one longer than the other four combined. Checked the numbers twice, consulted my clipboard, and even asked the fellow who relieves me on Thursdays.

He picked Oliver.

He's also been late four times this month.

The editor told me to make a confident selection. Said my job could depend on it.

I asked whether he'd considered how unreasonable that was.

He said to pick a winner.

The Pylon's Line: Lakota by 4.

If I'm wrong, I'll blame the relief worker. He was standing too close to the clipboard.

From the Editor's Desk

The editor reviewed my column before it went to print. Said the predictions were better, but the attitude still needed work.

I asked if he wanted me to be honest or positive.

He said both.

I told him he'd have to hire two people.

He's looking into it.

Last week: 3–2 outright, 3–2 against the lines. Performance Improvement Plan: Pending.
