The Pylon’s Lines · Week 8 Halloween Edition · October 31

By Gordie “The Pylon” MacNeil

Picks filed Halloween night. Lineup notes updated Sunday morning, November 1.

I went four for five last week. Stopped by the paper for my cheque and found a job posting in the window. My job. Figured it was a Halloween prank until I saw the closing date. “Experience preferred.” Seven weeks I’ve been doing this. How much experience did the previous fella have?

I mentioned the four winners. The editor mentioned I went three for five against the lines. I see he’s doing references himself.

Thursday’s already happened. I’m taking credit for none of it. These are my final winning margins, with those points already counted.

The Pick I’d Stake My Job On: Paul over Oliver

Matchup of the Week

Paul’s 6–1, leads 24.25–18.20, and still has Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Trey McBride to come. Last week he put up the league’s highest score. That’s a crew I’ll hire again. Oliver’s 5–2 and has Burrow throwing to Chase, which means one touchdown can arrive twice on my employment record. Chase is the man who could make me regret this. Paul by five and a half. Last week Oliver won by 6.70 after I picked him to win by seven and a half. I know exactly how much change that man owes me.

The Pick I’m Making With the Porch Light Off: Lakota over Carlie

The editor asked if I was taking Carlie a third time. I asked whether this was the interview. Golden and Kraft have already given her 36.20, against Lakota’s 17.00, and Bijan hasn’t started. There’s a perfectly respectable case for Carlie. I prefer Lakota’s Cook, Kyren, Lamb and Higgins, although starting both Goedert and LaPorta means I’m trusting two tight ends with my reputation. Lakota by one and a half. If this one goes wrong, I’d prefer people think I’m out.

Sarah over Ken. Hubbard’s 20.90 has Sarah ahead by 18.90, with Goff and Barkley still coming. As of Sunday morning, Brown is out and Sarah’s defence slot is empty. She’s got two spots to sort before kickoff. Ken has Lamar and Henry, so this isn’t charity. Sarah by two and a half. Ken’s welcome to ask Harrisons to beat the price.

Grant over Nick. Both are 4–3. Watson’s already supplied Grant with 15.30, and Jonathan Taylor is still coming. Nick has Allen, Puka and Jefferson, but the ruled-out Egbuka remains selected as I write. There’s time to replace him. Grant by four and a half. Nick squeezed all but two-tenths out of his lineup last week. I hope he enjoyed his coffee break.

Kyle over Luc. Coker’s given Kyle 8.60, and I like Gibbs and Bowers to carry it forward. Hall’s questionable, so Kyle needs to watch that before kickoff. Luc has Dak and St. Brown, and scored 150.10 last week without winning. Kyle by three and a half. Luc can keep the chair. I’m the one who needs a cushion.

Trade investigation

Eleven completed free-agent moves on the Week 8 sheet. No completed trades. Nobody’s exchanged players, picks or lunch money, so I’ve redirected the investigation toward the window.

It says the successful applicant must be available Thursdays. That rules me out, apparently, on the evidence.

Went back after dark. Took the posting down on my way home. Folded it up small enough to fit in my vest. I’ve got until Friday to find two references.

Matchup of the Week

Paul over Oliver

The Trading Block −5½
